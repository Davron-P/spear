About SPEAR

Students Prepared, Educated and Ready.

Our purpose

SPEAR Investment Banking is a student-run program at Babson College designed to prepare students for investment banking recruiting.

The program brings together technical education, professional development, mentorship, and practical experience. Students build the accounting and valuation knowledge, communication skills, and professional relationships needed during recruiting.

Our approach

Learning takes place through technical training, interview preparation, and networking. A cohort of 14 students works through 13 sessions, with opportunities to put concepts into practice.

The experience includes financial analysis, modeling, and a sell-side M&A capstone.

The Fall 2024 guiding principles emphasize consistent preparation, working together, honest feedback, and supporting one another.

Learn more about the program

Student leadership

SPEAR facilitators support students through technical education and recruiting preparation. Mentorship and alumni connections are part of the program experience.
