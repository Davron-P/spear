About SPEAR

Students prepared.
Educated. Ready.

A student-run program at Babson College, designed to help students turn an interest in investment banking into a foundation for recruiting.

Our purpose

Make preparation
count.

Investment banking recruiting calls for technical knowledge, clear communication, and strong professional relationships. SPEAR brings those areas together.

Through technical training, professional development, mentorship, and practical experience, students build the skills needed for interviews and internships.

A shared commitment
to getting better.

01 / Technical

Build your foundation.

Accounting, valuation, DCF analysis, M&A, and financial modeling.

02 / Recruiting

Prepare for the process.

Resume preparation, behavioral and technical interviews, mock interviews, and recruiting strategy.

03 / Networking

Build lasting connections.

Alumni connections, networking preparation, industry speakers, mentorship, and career guidance.
