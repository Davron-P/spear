SPEAR Investment Banking

About SPEAR

SPEAR stands for Students Prepared Educated and Ready. It is a student-run program at Babson College for students pursuing investment banking.

The program

Each semester, a cohort of 14 students completes 13 sessions led by facilitators with investment banking internship and recruiting experience.

Members study the technical material used in banking and apply it through financial modeling and an individual M&A capstone. They also receive guidance on recruiting and have opportunities to connect with other members and alumni working in the industry.

Explore the program →

Commitment

SPEAR requires a commitment comparable to a full academic course. Members are expected to come prepared, follow through on feedback, and take responsibility for their recruiting progress. Facilitators provide direct feedback and work closely with students throughout the semester.

Who can join

The program is designed for second-semester freshmen and first-semester sophomores, allowing students to prepare ahead of the investment banking recruiting cycle. Prior finance experience is not required.
