SPEAR Investment Banking

About SPEAR

Students Prepared Educated and Ready. A student-run program at Babson College, built from firsthand experience with investment banking recruiting.

Founded in spring 2021

How SPEAR began

Tyson Corner ’22 founded SPEAR after navigating investment banking recruiting and completing an internship himself. He brought the lessons, resources, and advice he had gathered into a structured program to help other Babson students prepare.

SPEAR was built from the ground up by students who understood the process and wanted to make it easier for the next class to find their footing.

Shaped by each class

Students who complete SPEAR return as facilitators and mentors, passing on what they have learned through recruiting and working in banking.

The program continues to change with them. Feedback from members and facilitators helps shape the curriculum, teaching, and recruiting preparation each semester so that SPEAR responds to what students need now.

Our guiding principles

What we ask of each other

Seek and act on feedback

Be honest about where you stand. Ask for feedback, give it thoughtfully, and use rejection as a reason to adjust and keep going.

Develop your own story

Take time to understand your experiences and what you want to pursue. Learn to explain that clearly and in your own words.

Support one another

Share what you know and help others prepare. Each member contributes to the group, and everyone has a part in its success.

Prepare consistently

Come ready for each session and follow through between meetings. Your preparation affects what the whole group can learn.

Who can join

The program is designed for second-semester freshmen and first-semester sophomores, allowing students to prepare ahead of the investment banking recruiting cycle. Prior finance experience is not required.
