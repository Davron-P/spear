About SPEAR

Students Prepared
Educated and Ready

A student-run investment banking preparation program at Babson College.

Our purpose

Preparation that goes
beyond the interview.

SPEAR brings together technical education, professional development, mentorship, and practical experience to prepare students for investment banking recruiting.

Students build accounting and valuation knowledge alongside the communication skills and professional relationships needed during recruiting.

Explore the program →

How we work

Prepare consistently.

A cohort of 14 students works through 13 sessions, putting financial concepts into practice.

Learn together.

Technical training, interview preparation, and networking are supported by student facilitators and alumni connections.

Put feedback to work.

The Fall 2024 guiding principles emphasize honest feedback, working together, and supporting one another.
