SPEAR Investment Banking

About SPEAR

Students Prepared Educated and Ready. A student-run program at Babson College, built from firsthand experience with investment banking recruiting.

Founded in spring 2021

How SPEAR began

Tyson Corner ’22 knew he wanted to work in investment banking, but finding the right preparation took years of searching for résumé templates, interview questions, and technical resources. After completing a banking internship, he used that experience and feedback from industry professionals to create SPEAR.

Launched at Babson in spring 2021, the 13-week program brought technical training, financial modeling, and recruiting preparation into one student-led course. It gave students a clearer way to prepare for a recruiting cycle that starts early in college, with peers working toward the same goal.

Shaped by each class

From the beginning, participants have returned to teach the next cohort. Joyce Wang ’23 joined SPEAR’s first class and later became a facilitator, helping other students through the same preparation that had helped her secure an investment banking internship.

That experience continues to inform the program. Facilitators bring back what they learn in interviews and internships, while members share feedback throughout the semester. Each class helps refine the sessions, materials, and recruiting guidance to reflect current needs.

What began as one student’s effort to share what he had learned has grown into a community where members can learn from peers and stay connected with alumni working in banking.

Who can join

The program is designed for second-semester freshmen and first-semester sophomores, allowing students to prepare ahead of the investment banking recruiting cycle. Prior finance experience is not required.
