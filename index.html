SPEAR is a student-run program preparing Babson students for investment banking through technical training, practical experience, and mentorship.

Build your foundation.

Accounting, valuation, DCF analysis, M&A, and financial modeling.

Build lasting connections.

Alumni connections, networking preparation, industry speakers, mentorship, and career guidance.
