Investment Banking

A student-run program preparing Babson students for careers in investment banking.

Through technical education, recruiting preparation, and mentorship, SPEAR helps students develop the skills required for interviews and internships.

About SPEAR

The program

SPEAR’s 13-session program combines technical training, recruiting preparation, and networking in a cohort of 14 students.

Students apply their learning through financial analysis, valuation and modeling, interview practice, and a sell-side M&A capstone.
