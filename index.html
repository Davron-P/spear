01 / ABOUT

STUDENTS PREPARED, EDUCATED AND READY. →

SPEAR Investment Banking is a student-run program at Babson College designed to prepare students for the investment banking recruiting process.

Through technical training, professional development, mentorship, and practical experience, SPEAR helps students build the skills necessary to succeed in investment banking.
