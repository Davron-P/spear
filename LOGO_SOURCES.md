## Rendering
The SVG assets contain vector paths, with no embedded raster images or font dependencies. The Rothschild & Co horizontal symbol is extracted from the official SVG sprite. White Moelis, PJT, and William Blair marks are rendered dark with CSS for contrast. Other artwork retains its source colors.

Cantor uses the official 263 × 48 PNG at a displayed width of 130 CSS pixels. It provides approximately 2× pixel density at that size. Logo proportions are preserved, with individual sizing and optical alignment in styles.css.
