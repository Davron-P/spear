# SPEAR visual guidelines

## Brand
- Calibri, Arial, and Helvetica are the system-font stack.
- Navy (#1d3155) is used for headings and primary actions.
- Muted green (#80a27b) is used for accents.
- The charcoal footer (#172126) uses white text and a white LinkedIn icon.
- The SPEAR wordmark appears in the site header.

## Layout
The site has five pages: Home, About, Program, People, and Apply. Investment banking placements appear on the homepage. Shared navigation, footer, and button styles are defined in styles.css.

## Accessibility
Navigation identifies the current page. Content links use boxed buttons. Keyboard focus remains visible. The placement carousel pauses on hover or focus and becomes a static layout when reduced motion is enabled.

## Images
Keep photographs in their original proportions where possible. Use accurate alternative text. Firm logos use vector artwork where available and individual display sizes to balance their different proportions.
