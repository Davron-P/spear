# SPEAR design reference review

Reviewed September 6, 2026. This is a design comparison, not a ranking of finance clubs. Club outcomes are self-reported; no comparable audited club-level placement-rate dataset was identified.

## References

- Michigan Investment Banking Club: https://www.themibc.org/ and https://www.themibc.org/about-us . The homepage uses a real Ross building photograph beside a white text area, serif type, a direct organization name, and conventional page links. Its About page describes education, experience, and career preparation. Its stated 100% placement rate is an annual goal, not a verified outcome. Alumni reference: https://www.themibc.org/alumni .
- Cornell Finance Club: https://cornellfinanceclub.org/ . The homepage uses serif typography, conventional top navigation, a photographic setting, and a firm strip. https://cornellfinanceclub.org/placement/ lists outcomes by class; the Class of 2028 includes five Evercore and four J.P. Morgan placements. These are club-published counts, not independently audited rates.
- Wharton Undergraduate Finance Club: https://www.whartonundergradfinance.com/ . The site emphasizes the organization name, campus photography, leadership, community, and recruitment. Its recruitment page gives specific events and dates: https://www.whartonundergradfinance.com/f26-recruitment . Penn's club directory links this domain: https://pennclubs.com/club/wharton-undergraduate-finance-club/ .
- Mutual Investment Club of Cornell: https://www.cornellmicc.com/ . The homepage includes alumni firms and class-specific junior placements, demonstrating that outcomes can sit on the homepage without a separate placements page. Club-published placements include roles at Morgan Stanley, J.P. Morgan, and Moelis.
- Cornell Investment Banking Club: https://www.cornell-ib.com/ and https://www.cornell-ib.com/recruitment . Content emphasizes club identity, real community photographs, and a practical recruitment timeline.

## Applied decisions

- Use the actual SPEAR name as the homepage headline and direct descriptive headings on inner pages.
- Preserve separate About, Program, People, and Apply pages; link Placements to the homepage section from every page.
- Use restrained serif headings, readable system-font body text, white space, thin dividers, navy text, and limited green accents.
- Replace oversized motivational headlines, decorative arrows, numbered feature cards, the large statistics panel, and repeated promotional banners with straightforward prose, curriculum lists, and a compact program facts table.
- Use a real Babson campus photograph with a caption and source, rather than generated people or invented event photos.
- Retain the nine existing SPEAR firms without inventing counts, sponsorship relationships, or placement rates.
- Keep unavailable application and facilitator information honest until confirmed details are supplied.
