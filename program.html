Inside the curriculum

From foundations
to practice.

Explore the areas covered throughout the program.

01Technical education

Develop the financial knowledge required for investment banking interviews and internships.

  • Accounting and financial modeling
  • Valuation and discounted cash flow analysis
  • Mergers and acquisitions
02Recruiting preparation

Prepare for applications and interviews through guided practice and direct feedback.

  • Resume preparation and recruiting strategy
  • Behavioral and technical interviews
  • Mock interviews
03Networking

Learn to approach professional conversations and build relationships across the industry.

  • Alumni connections and industry speakers
  • Outreach and conversation preparation
  • Thoughtful questions and follow-up
  • Mentorship and career guidance
04Practical experience

Apply financial analysis, valuation, and modeling in a sell-side M&A capstone. Technical quizzes and interview practice are paired with feedback, reflection, and commitments to action.
