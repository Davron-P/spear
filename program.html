SPEAR Investment Banking

The program

Technical education, recruiting preparation, and networking for Babson students pursuing investment banking.

13 sessions   ·   14 students per cohort

Technical education

Develop the financial knowledge required for investment banking interviews and internships.

  • Accounting
  • Valuation
  • Discounted cash flow analysis
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Financial modeling

Recruiting preparation

Prepare for the application and interview process through resume work, interview practice, and recruiting guidance.

  • Resume preparation
  • Behavioral interviews
  • Technical interviews
  • Mock interviews
  • Recruiting strategy

Networking

Learn how to approach professional conversations and build relationships across the industry.

  • Alumni connections
  • Networking preparation
  • Industry speakers
  • Mentorship
  • Career guidance

Learning through practice

SPEAR’s Fall 2024 materials pair technical quizzes and interview practice with direct feedback, reflection, and commitments to action.

Sessions move from preparation and warm-up into practical learning, followed by a debrief and specific next steps. Networking work includes outreach, preparing for conversations, asking thoughtful questions, and following up.

Practical experience

Apply concepts through financial analysis, valuation and modeling, interview preparation, and networking. The sell-side M&A capstone brings technical work into a practical setting.
